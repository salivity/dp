What Is the Maximum Raw Bandwidth of DisplayPort 1.0?

DisplayPort 1.0 delivers a maximum raw bandwidth of 10.8 Gbps across a standard four-lane connection. Introduced by VESA in May 2006 to succeed legacy display standards like VGA and DVI, the specification established the foundation for modern digital display interfaces. Factoring in its 8b/10b line-coding overhead, DisplayPort 1.0 provides an effective maximum data rate of 8.64 Gbps, which is sufficient to drive resolutions up to 2560 × 1600 at 60 Hz.

Lane Architecture and Bandwidth Breakdown

DisplayPort relies on a micro-packet architecture that transmits data across scalable differential signaling pairs known as lanes. A full-bandwidth DisplayPort cable contains four main-link lanes, though devices can negotiate down to two lanes or a single lane when lower bandwidth is required.

In the 1.0 specification, VESA defined two distinct link rates:

  • Reduced Bit Rate (RBR): Operates at 1.62 Gbps per lane, resulting in a total raw bandwidth of 6.48 Gbps across four lanes.
  • High Bit Rate (HBR): Operates at 2.70 Gbps per lane, yielding the maximum raw bandwidth of 10.80 Gbps across four lanes.

Because DisplayPort 1.0 uses 8b/10b encoding—where every 8 bits of actual data require 10 physical bits of transmission to ensure DC balance and clock synchronization—20% of the raw transmission is consumed by protocol overhead. This reduces the maximum payload data throughput from 10.80 Gbps down to an effective 8.64 Gbps.

Supported Display Resolutions

With an effective data throughput of 8.64 Gbps, DisplayPort 1.0 accommodated the high-end display requirements of the mid-2000s using standard 24-bit RGB (8 bits per channel) color:

  • 1080p (1920 × 1080) at 60 Hz: Requires approximately 3.20 Gbps of data bandwidth.
  • 1440p (2560 × 1440) at 60 Hz: Requires approximately 5.63 Gbps of data bandwidth.
  • WQXGA (2560 × 1600) at 60 Hz: Requires approximately 6.88 Gbps of data bandwidth.

DisplayPort 1.0 comfortably handled high-resolution computer monitors of its era without compression, serving as the baseline capability before DisplayPort 1.2 arrived to double transmission rates to 21.60 Gbps.
