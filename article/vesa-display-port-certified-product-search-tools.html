VESA DisplayPort Certified Product Search Tools

The Video Electronics Standards Association (VESA) maintains an official, publicly accessible verification database that allows consumers, system integrators, and manufacturers to verify genuine DisplayPort hardware. This guide details the specific online tools VESA provides, how the certification registry functions, and the exact steps to locate certified cables, displays, adapters, and source devices to avoid non-compliant hardware.

The Official DisplayPort Certified Products Database

The primary verification resource maintained by VESA is the DisplayPort Certified Products Database, hosted on the official DisplayPort website (displayport.org). Because VESA requires all certified products to undergo rigorous testing at an authorized test center (ATC) or through an approved self-testing procedure, every compliant product is indexed directly in this central catalog.

This tool serves as the single source of truth for standard DisplayPort, Mini DisplayPort, and DisplayPort over USB Type-C implementations.

How to Use the Verification Tool

The DisplayPort search portal provides multiple query fields to cross-reference retail packaging and marketing claims against official test records:

  • Product Category: Users can filter specifically for Cables, Monitors, Graphics Cards, Laptops, Motherboards, Adapters, or Docks.
  • Manufacturer / Brand: Allows searching by the original design manufacturer (ODM) or the retail brand name.
  • Model Name or Number: Enables exact queries using retail SKU or model designations found on product packaging.
  • DisplayPort Standard / Performance Tier: Users can isolate products meeting specific performance thresholds, such as legacy DisplayPort 1.4 standards or the newer DisplayPort 2.1 Ultra-high Bit Rate (UHBR) tiers.

Verifying Cables and UHBR Certification

A crucial function of the database is verifying cable performance. With the rollout of DisplayPort 2.1, VESA established certified performance tiers, notably DP40 and DP80:

  • DP40 Cables: Certified to support up to UHBR10 (10 Gbps per lane, 40 Gbps aggregate throughput).
  • DP80 Cables: Certified to support up to UHBR20 (20 Gbps per lane, 80 Gbps aggregate throughput).

The verification tool lets users check whether a cable advertised as "DP 2.1" or "8K/16K" has legitimately passed VESA compliance testing, ensuring proper shielding, signal integrity, and pin assignment (particularly avoiding the pin-20 power delivery defect found in uncertified cables).

Complementary VESA Verification Tools

In addition to the main DisplayPort database, VESA maintains specialized compliance directories for display performance standards built around DisplayPort technology:

  • DisplayHDR Database: A registry listing monitors and laptops certified under the VESA DisplayHDR and DisplayHDR True Black specifications (e.g., DisplayHDR 400, 600, 1000).
  • ClearMR Database: A directory for products certified under the VESA ClearMR standard, which measures digital display motion blur performance.

By consulting these integrated registries, users can confirm that their video sources, cables, and displays comply with official VESA interoperability and performance benchmarks.
