DisplayPort HBR, HBR2, and HBR3 Data Rates Explained

DisplayPort interfaces rely on standardized transmission modes to scale bandwidth across audio and video connections. This guide breaks down the precise data rates of High Bit Rate (HBR), High Bit Rate 2 (HBR2), and High Bit Rate 3 (HBR3), detailing both the raw signaling rates and the net effective payload capacities across single-lane and full four-lane configurations.

Understanding DisplayPort Signaling and Encoding

DisplayPort transmission rates are categorized into two primary figures: the raw bit rate (the raw physical symbol clock frequency) and the effective data rate (the actual bandwidth available for uncompressed video, audio, and metadata).

HBR, HBR2, and HBR3 use an 8b/10b encoding scheme. In this scheme, every 8 bits of actual data are encoded into a 10-bit symbol to ensure DC balance and clock recovery. This introduces a 20% transmission overhead, meaning the maximum effective payload is exactly 80% of the raw bit rate.

HBR (High Bit Rate)

Introduced in the DisplayPort 1.1 standard (and retained in subsequent versions), HBR doubled the transfer speed of the original RBR (Reduced Bit Rate) specification.

  • Raw Bit Rate:
    • Per Lane: 2.70 Gbps (Gigabits per second)
    • 4-Lane Link: 10.80 Gbps
  • Effective Data Rate (Payload):
    • Per Lane: 2.16 Gbps
    • 4-Lane Link: 8.64 Gbps
  • Typical Display Capabilities: Supports 1080p up to 144Hz or 2560×1440 (1440p) at 60Hz using standard 8-bit color.

HBR2 (High Bit Rate 2)

Introduced with DisplayPort 1.2, HBR2 doubled the bandwidth of HBR to facilitate early 4K displays, multi-stream transport (daisy chaining), and stereoscopic 3D.

  • Raw Bit Rate:
    • Per Lane: 5.40 Gbps
    • 4-Lane Link: 21.60 Gbps
  • Effective Data Rate (Payload):
    • Per Lane: 4.32 Gbps
    • 4-Lane Link: 17.28 Gbps
  • Typical Display Capabilities: Supports uncompressed 4K (3840×2160) at 60Hz or 1440p at up to 165Hz with 8-bit color depth.

HBR3 (High Bit Rate 3)

Defined in DisplayPort 1.3 and carried forward as the primary mode for DisplayPort 1.4, HBR3 accommodates high-refresh-rate 4K, HDR, and 8K displays (often utilizing Display Stream Compression, or DSC).

  • Raw Bit Rate:
    • Per Lane: 8.10 Gbps
    • 4-Lane Link: 32.40 Gbps
  • Effective Data Rate (Payload):
    • Per Lane: 6.48 Gbps
    • 4-Lane Link: 25.92 Gbps
  • Typical Display Capabilities: Supports uncompressed 4K at 120Hz (8-bit), uncompressed 5K at 60Hz, or 8K at 60Hz when combined with DSC 1.2.
